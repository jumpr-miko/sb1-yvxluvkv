Workflow alignment meeting focused on referral payouts and Salesforce integration rules.

Primary referral payout processes and tracking
* Primary referral payouts operate through separate cash and bet credit streams that cannot be combined into a single payout record.
* Cash payouts require tracking the date sent and the BMO ID for auditing purposes.
* Bet credit payouts require chunking the bulk amount into smaller deposit allocations across specific operators.
* Payout records are restricted to a single currency at a time, inherited directly from the signup currencies.
* Completing a payout record automatically updates the associated signups to remove them from the active payment queue.


Secondary referral thresholds and discretionary management
* Secondary referral bonuses trigger after reaching 10 eligible signups, offering 100 in cash or 200 in bet credit.
* Secondary referral payouts are managed at the discretion of the rep using an eligibility flag on the referer account.


Financial reporting scope and system integrations
* Financial reporting tracks overall cash flow, encompassing player deposits, redeposits, and BA commissions.
* Phase 1 prioritizes Salesforce for data collection while maintaining existing internal tools for commission calculations due to the hard November 1 deadline.


Salesforce workflow and player payout tracking
* Handled player payouts outside of Salesforce for the current phase without separate complex workflows.
* Tracked deposit and signup records directly on individual signup entries within Salesforce.
* Captured actual deposit amounts, dates, and screenshots only when players submitted their deposit information.


Fraud management and payout batch adjustments
* Blocked confirmed fraudulent signups from being added to active payout batches while keeping them visible.
* Locked payout records once marked as paid to prevent modifications by representatives.
* Permitted representatives to remove unconfirmed or flagged signups from unpaid payout batches.


Minimum thresholds for referral commissions
* Established that primary referers have no minimum signup thresholds before qualifying for commissions.
* Required BAs to achieve a minimum of 30 signups initially and during each payout run.


Incomplete redeposits and exception handling
* Tracked incomplete or failed redeposits at the individual redeposit record level rather than modifying payout statuses.
