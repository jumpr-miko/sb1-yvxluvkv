Details
* Meeting Objectives and Introductions: Kobi Kunasekaran, Ali Nawaz, Andy Bhiekham, and Jay convened to review the user registration process, operator logging systems, and the data connectivity between agency sites and AirTable, as Kobi Kunasekaran sought to understand how these systems link to the company’s internal operations (00:00:02).
* Logging and Spreadsheet System: Ali Nawaz explained that the team transitioned from manual tracking to a Google Sheets system to manage operator signups, noting that each operator has unique deals and target CPAs that the operations team must monitor (00:01:39).
* Operational Context and Volume Distribution: Ali Nawaz and Waleed Alasadi discussed the distribution of sales volume among Brand Ambassadors (BAS), noting that the team assigns operators and manages deals to meet specific volume targets while maintaining fair distribution among reps (00:04:26).
* Verification and Wagering Audits: Ali Nawaz outlined the verification process, which requires reps to collect welcome emails and wager/transaction screenshots to audit player activity and ensure they meet operator-specific requirements before a CPA is triggered (00:06:09).
* CMS for Operator Management: Ali Nawaz and Jay introduced the team to PlayCan CMS, the platform used to turn operator links on or off for individual reps; they emphasized that BAS do not have access to this system (00:08:49).
* Brand Ambassadors and Organizational Structure: Jay clarified that the company uses a branding model where managers like Ali Nawaz and Andy Bhiekham own brand identities (e.g., Ashurix) and manage specific reps who operate under those brands using dedicated, trackable links (00:10:43).
* Link Tracking and URL Slugs: Jay and Ali Nawaz explained that each rep is assigned unique links and URL slugs, which allows the company to trace signups back to specific Brand Ambassadors, provided the reps use the correct assigned links (00:16:02).
* AirTable as the Source of Truth: The group established that AirTable functions as the primary database for managing active links, tracking ranges, and effective dates, acting as a critical fail-safe for the PlayCan CMS (00:17:43).
* Challenges with Manual Logging: Ali Nawaz noted that historical data often contains discrepancies due to rep mislogging when they are switched between operators mid-month, necessitating manual reviews of timestamps and welcome emails to correct the logs (00:21:43).
* Lead Generation and Salesforce: Kobi Kunasekaran and Ali Nawaz discussed the distinction between tracking clicks and actual conversions, noting that the current process heavily relies on screenshots provided by reps via WhatsApp rather than just automated data (00:23:36).
* Seasonality and Volume Management: Ali Nawaz indicated that the number of active books and volume availability fluctuate throughout the year, with peak activity typically occurring during the NFL season (00:26:09).
* Link Assignment Logic: Jay and Kobi Kunasekaran discussed the importance of unique links for tracking "erosion" (lost data), though they acknowledged that limited supply from operators sometimes forces multiple reps to share a single link, which complicates attribution (00:27:49).
* Data Attribution and Reporting: Jay and Ali Nawaz confirmed that when links are shared, tracking individual rep performance becomes more difficult, and the team often has to manually investigate logs to determine which rep drove the signup (00:30:47).
* System Integration and Gaps: The group discussed the lack of native signup attribution in the current CMS, and while they are looking toward Salesforce to better track leads, historical data gaps remain a concern (00:33:12).
* Operator Switching Workflow: Ali Nawaz explained that when a rep is switched from one operator to another, they must reference welcome emails to log signups correctly based on the date of the switch, as some leads may have been generated prior to the transition (00:35:51).
* Managing Link Statuses in AirTable: The team reviewed how AirTable manages the "active" versus "paused" status of links and highlighted how the platform handles multi-state assignments, such as distinct links for different regions (00:37:34).
* Bot-Assisted Link Management: Andy Bhiekham described how the team utilizes a bot to handle link assignments, which helps optimize resource usage by automatically identifying the most suitable available link for a rep (00:38:59).
* Workflow for Updating Assignments: Andy Bhiekham detailed the process where the bot suggests a reassignment, the team confirms the change in AirTable, and then they manually update the link in the PlayCan CMS (00:43:02).
* Volume Caps and Status Management: Jay and Andy Bhiekham confirmed that links are paused rather than deleted in AirTable when volume caps are reached or deals conclude, allowing for future reactivation (00:45:12).
* Manual Reassignment Procedures: Jay demonstrated how to manually reassign an existing link code to a different rep within AirTable, effectively pausing the link for the previous holder and activating it for the new one (00:47:00).
* Reporting and Historical Data: Kobi Kunasekaran raised concerns regarding the loss of historical visibility on pause and resume dates, while Andy Bhiekham noted that while effective dates are tracked, the system's ability to show a detailed history of pauses may be limited (00:49:04).
* Assignment Tracking and Priority Books: Jay explained that the "Previous Assignment" field tracks changes within the same casino and that "Priority Books" are designated based on profitability and the company's relationship with the operator (00:53:00).
* Communication and Logging Errors: Jay and Ali Nawaz noted that reps are notified of link changes via WhatsApp, but acknowledged that this manual communication channel is a frequent source of logging errors (00:55:50).
* Compliance Requirements: Andy Bhiekham clarified that as part of the compliance process, all clients are required to complete a survey regarding their experience with the platform (00:57:59).
* User Survey Requirement: The team discussed the necessity for all users to complete a survey prior to participating in an iGaming study. Kobi Kunasekaran sought clarification on whether the survey requirement was per person or per study, and Andy Bhiekham confirmed that the survey is required once per user account. Once a user completes the survey, they are eligible to participate in multiple studies without needing to complete the survey again (00:58:45).
* Next Steps and Communication Channels: Kobi Kunasekaran expressed that the meeting resolved outstanding questions and invited the team to share any challenges or improvement suggestions to assist in the final Salesforce design. Ali Nawaz agreed to provide support through calls or messages as needed. The participants agreed to utilize Slack for ongoing communication, with Kobi Kunasekaran offering to establish a dedicated channel for the group (00:59:32).
