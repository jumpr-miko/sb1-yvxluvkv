Summary
Discussion of data architecture strategy and Salesforce storage optimization and cost analysis for long-term project planning.

Data Storage Strategy
External operator data storage will utilize a Postgres instance to optimize costs over Snowflake. This architecture effectively handles the estimated 5 to 10 gigabytes of annual data requirements.

Signup Process Optimization
Replacing the Lead object with person accounts and dedicated signup records streamlines user tracking. Identity matching logic prioritizes email verification, using manual review for edge cases to ensure accuracy.

Infrastructure Cost Evaluation
Transitioning storage from Dropbox to Google Cloud Platform aligns with existing ecosystem goals to reduce expenditures. Salesforce storage pricing is under review to leverage potential partner-level discounts.
